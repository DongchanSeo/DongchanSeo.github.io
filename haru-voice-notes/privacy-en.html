Haru Voice Notes — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Android app com.haru.voicenotes

Records kept on your device

Microphone permission is used for recordings you start while the app is on screen, for up to 10 minutes. Audio, titles, written notes, transcripts, summaries, creation times, durations and processing states remain in app-private local storage. Each note keeps its chosen Korean or English language. Written notes remain available if microphone permission is denied. One email recipient is saved in local settings and can be cleared separately.

Adult confirmation

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It stores only your local confirmation that you are at least 18. It does not request a birth date, identity details or Google account information for this confirmation. This is not an identity or age verification service. Resetting confirmation requires confirmation again and does not delete saved recordings or notes. Google Play handles its account-based restriction of minor access.

On-device AI and Google services

Supported devices use official ML Kit features for Korean and English transcription and summaries. ML Kit states that processing inputs and outputs remain on the device. Haru provides no external LLM or developer-server fallback. Android/Google services may use the network to prepare models and configuration.

SDK diagnostics and usage information

Google documents collection of device/app details, user, device or other identifiers, installation identifiers, performance/configuration/size/event/error information and configured languages. Google uses metrics for API diagnosis, maintenance, improvement and misuse detection. SDK transmission uses HTTPS. This is separate from locally processed note content. Google policies linked below govern its handling.

Drafts handed to your email app

After a separate notice and consent, email preparation hands the recipient, title, and summary or written note to your chosen external email app. If no summary or written note exists, the body says none is saved; transcripts are not automatically included. Transcript and audio attachments are optional. Choosing audio grants the selected email app permission to read the attachment. Haru does not send mail automatically. You review the recipient, content and attachments and press Send yourself. The external app may sync drafts before sending and its own policies apply.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device while you keep them. Confirming deletion in the list or details removes that note’s audio, title, written text, transcript and summary. Finish or cancel active work first. If confirmed deletion is interrupted, the app completes only previously confirmed cleanup on a later launch. A content-free identifier guard prevents old processing results from restoring deleted content and remains until app data is cleared or the app is removed. Deleting a note does not clear the separately saved recipient or remove external copies. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records. App settings exclude backup and device transfer, but manufacturer and operating-system behavior may vary. Local deletion does not guarantee deletion of Google SDK metrics or external service copies.

Operator and contact

Operator: Dongchan Seo. Support and privacy contact: eternalamour@naver.com. When you contact the operator, your sender address and message are used to answer and resolve your inquiry. Inquiry information is retained while needed and removed when no longer needed, except where a legal retention obligation applies. You do not need to attach your recordings or notes to an inquiry. The app has no ads, user accounts or automatic uploads of records to a developer server.
