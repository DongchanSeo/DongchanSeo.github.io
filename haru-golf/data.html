The September 20, 2026 snapshot contains 30,838 named golf-course entries. Unnamed entries and clearly labeled driving ranges or miniature/park golf were excluded; all 572 original Korean catalog IDs were retained for record compatibility. Display coordinates are OSM points or geometry centers, not surveyed entrance locations. Country and region labels use geographic boundary matching. This is not a complete or verified list of operating courses.
